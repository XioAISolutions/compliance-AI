# Ontario Securities Commission — Notice of Deficiency

**Compliance Review — Exempt Market Dealer**

File: 2026-EMD-0417-CW
From: Ontario Securities Commission, Compliance and Registrant Regulation Branch
To: Chief Compliance Officer, Crosswater Securities Inc.
Date: April 14, 2026

Response required by: **May 12, 2026**.

---

Dear Chief Compliance Officer,

Staff of the Ontario Securities Commission ("OSC") recently completed a
compliance field review of Crosswater Securities Inc. covering the period
January 1, 2024 through December 31, 2025. This deficiency letter sets out
our preliminary findings and the matters on which we require your response.

Please provide a written response addressing each of the items below, with
supporting documentation where indicated, by the response required date
above.

## 1. Suitability documentation

Staff reviewed a sample of 25 accredited investor files opened during the
review period. In 7 of those files, we were unable to locate the current
Know Your Client record required under section 13.2 of National Instrument
31-103. Please describe the steps Crosswater has taken to remediate these
files and to prevent recurrence.

## 2. Marketing communications

The March 2025 investor pitch deck for Northstar Growth Fund II includes
annualized returns for Fund I without clearly labelling them as past
performance under section 13.15 of NI 31-103 and related CSA Staff Notice
31-325. Please explain the review and approval process Crosswater uses for
sales communications, and provide copies of all marketing materials
distributed in the past 12 months.

## 3. Relationship disclosure information

Please confirm the date on which Crosswater last refreshed its relationship
disclosure information and describe how the current version is provided to
new clients prior to the first trade.

## 4. Anti-money laundering program

Please provide a copy of Crosswater's current written AML / anti-terrorist
financing policies and procedures, and describe how FINTRAC-reportable
transactions are monitored and escalated.

---

We remind you that a response is required by **May 12, 2026**. Failure to
respond by the deadline may result in the matter being referred to the
Enforcement Branch.

If you have any questions please contact the undersigned.
