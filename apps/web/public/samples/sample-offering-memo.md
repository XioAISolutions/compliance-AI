# Northstar Growth Fund II — Offering Memorandum

**Form 45-106F2 Offering Memorandum** — Non-Qualifying Issuer (Ontario)

Dated: March 14, 2026

---

## The Offering

Northstar Growth Fund II LP (the "Fund") is offering Class A limited partnership
units by way of private placement to accredited investors pursuant to the
accredited investor prospectus exemption in section 2.3 of National Instrument
45-106 *Prospectus Exemptions* ("NI 45-106"). This Offering Memorandum has been
prepared solely for the use of prospective accredited investors and is not a
prospectus. No securities commission has reviewed this document.

**Subscription price:** CAD $100,000 per Class A Unit (minimum one Unit).
**Maximum offering:** CAD $50,000,000.
**Minimum offering:** None.
**Closings:** Rolling, at the General Partner's discretion.

## Use of Proceeds

The Fund intends to deploy subscription proceeds substantially as follows:

- ~75% into early-stage Canadian technology companies, targeting Series A and
  Series B rounds with cheque sizes of $2–6 million.
- ~15% held in reserve for follow-on rounds in existing portfolio companies.
- ~10% to cover the Fund's organizational expenses, management fee, and
  ongoing administration.

Any material deviation from the planned use of proceeds requires the written
consent of the Limited Partner Advisory Committee.

## Risk Factors

An investment in Class A Units is speculative and involves a high degree of
risk. Prospective investors should carefully consider the following risk
factors, among others, before subscribing:

- **Illiquidity.** Class A Units are not listed on any exchange and there is
  no secondary market. Transfer is restricted under the Limited Partnership
  Agreement and applicable securities laws.
- **Concentration.** The Fund's portfolio will be concentrated in Canadian
  private technology companies, and returns may be materially affected by the
  performance of a small number of investments.
- **Key person risk.** The Fund depends on the continued involvement of the
  General Partner's founding investment team.
- **Regulatory risk.** Changes to NI 45-106 or related companion policies
  could restrict the Fund's ability to raise capital or require additional
  filings.

## Rights of Action

Ontario investors have, in addition to any other rights they may have at law,
a statutory right of action for damages or rescission against the issuer in
the event that this Offering Memorandum contains a misrepresentation, as
more particularly described in Schedule A.

## Subscription Agreement

Each prospective investor must execute the subscription agreement attached as
Schedule B and deliver it, together with the required accredited investor
certification, to the General Partner at least two (2) business days prior
to the intended closing date.
